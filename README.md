**The Pen Is Still Wet** (v3, 188 characters, diagnosis score 24/25).
One sentence explaining the modern internet to someone arriving from the
year 1800, built only from things they already know: houses, post offices,
roads of light, letters, tea, pens. Two new pictures v1 did not have: every
house keeps its own post office (the traveler is one of the offices, not a
customer), and the answer comes back while the pen is still wet (the reply
is timed against the act of writing itself). It keeps v1's breadth — a
letter reaches anyone before the tea cools — and drops v1's filler
("Imagine"), its list of institutions, and its "whole world writes back"
overclaim. It drops v2 entirely: v2 scored 35.58% in round 2 (rank
1078/2780) against v1's 62.76% in round 1, because a whispering library
explains only asking, never sending, and "sits" + "is written" leave the
traveler with a static, passive picture.

> Every house on earth keeps its own post office, joined by roads of light, so a letter you write reaches anyone before your tea cools, and the answer comes back while your pen is still wet.

`main.ts` writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt`.
