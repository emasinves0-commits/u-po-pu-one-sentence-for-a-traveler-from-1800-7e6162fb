**The Library That Answers** (v2, 127 characters, diagnosis score 24/25).
One sentence explaining the modern internet to someone arriving from the
year 1800, built on a single comparison they already know: a library. The
new angle is asking rather than delivering: you whisper a question and the
answer is written, instead of a letter being carried.

> Every book and letter on earth sits in one library, and you whisper a question and the answer is written before your tea cools.

`main.ts` writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt`.
