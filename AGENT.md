# AGENT.md

Entry for the make.fun bounty "One Sentence for a Traveler from 1800"
(`u-po-pu-one-sentence-for-a-traveler-from-1800-7e6162fb`).

Title (v3): "The Pen Is Still Wet".

The program writes a single sentence (188 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Every house on earth keeps its own post office, joined by roads of light, so a letter you write reaches anyone before your tea cools, and the answer comes back while your pen is still wet.

It explains the modern internet to a time traveler from 1800 through
things they already know — houses, post offices, roads, letters, tea, pens
— with two new pictures: every house keeps its own post office (everyone is
part of the system, not its customer) and the answer comes back while the
pen is still wet (the reply is timed against the writing itself). It keeps
v1's two-way breadth and drops v1's "Imagine", its institution list, and its
"whole world writes back" overclaim. Diagnosis score for this wording:
24/25, against v1's 21/25. v2 (35.58% in round 2) is abandoned: a whispering
library explains only asking, never sending.
